First-person [b]helmet cameras[/b] for Door Kickers 2. Watch the mission or replay through an operator's eyes in a small picture in the corner, live, while the main view keeps working as normal.

Everything is configurable through the [url=https://github.com/gabrweu/doorkickers-modloader]Modloader[/url] menu or through the helmet button in the HUD. [b]Requires the Modloader[/b]: see Installation below.

[h1]Features[/h1]

[b]Helmet cam on your operators[/b]
Works in missions and in replays, and follows pause, speed and skipping exactly. A fallen operator's cam goes black.

[b]Quick controls[/b]
[list]
    [*] Hold [b]H[/b] to open or close the cam. The key can be changed.
    [*] Tap [b]H[/b] to switch to the next operator.
    [*] The operator's name sits on the picture, in the colour of their path.
[/list]

[b]Automatic switching[/b]
The cam follows your selection: it jumps to whoever you select, either at once or when you tap the key.
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Petellius/dk2-workshop-media/main/helmet-cam/auto-switch.gif[/img]

[b]Action cam[/b]
Follows the fight: when the operator you're watching stops shooting or aiming, the cam switches to another operator who is in combat.
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Petellius/dk2-workshop-media/main/helmet-cam/action-cam.gif[/img]

[b]HUD menu[/b]
Click the helmet icon in the HUD bar to open a small menu of quick toggles: selection mode, bodycam and fisheye effects, action cam and night vision. Also available in replays.
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Petellius/dk2-workshop-media/main/helmet-cam/icon-menu.png[/img]

[b]Clean view[/b]
The picture hides plans, paths, door and wall highlights and, optionally, the night vision overlay and IR lasers, so it looks like a real helmet cam.
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Petellius/dk2-workshop-media/main/helmet-cam/hide-in-game-ui.png[/img]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Petellius/dk2-workshop-media/main/helmet-cam/night-vision.gif[/img]

[b]Your picture, your way[/b]
Pick the corner, the offset and the size of the picture, plus field of view and camera position. Optional cams for enemies and civilians too.
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Petellius/dk2-workshop-media/main/helmet-cam/config-options.jpg[/img]

[b]The helmet cam renders the scene a second time while it is open, so it costs frame rate.[/b] A refresh rate setting (30 fps by default) keeps it light, and nothing extra is drawn while the cam is closed, the game is paused or a menu is open.

[h1]Installation[/h1]
[olist]
    [*] Install the [url=https://github.com/gabrweu/doorkickers-modloader/releases]Modloader[/url]: close the game, download the release, and copy [b]dk2ml.dll[/b] and [b]dbghelp.dll[/b] into the game's folder (Steam: right-click Door Kickers 2 > Manage > Browse local files).
    [*] Subscribe to this mod, start the game and enable it in the Mods menu.
    [*] [b]Quit the game completely and start it again through Steam.[/b] The restart option in the Mods menu does not load DLL mods.
    [*] When Windows asks about loading external code, choose Continue.
[/olist]
If you already use the Modloader for another mod, such as era's Free Camera, step 1 is done. Mods with code can run anything on your PC, so only install mods from sources you trust.
